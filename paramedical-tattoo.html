Paramedical Tattoo

Paramedical work, and the kind that restores

Fine-line body tattoo, and paramedical work for clients rebuilding after surgery, injury or illness. Both are done in the same hygiene-controlled room, by certified hands.

Book a consultation

What we do

Fine-line and small-scale decorative tattoo, designed with you rather than picked off a wall. And paramedical tattoo: areola and 3D nipple restoration after breast surgery, scar camouflage, stretch-mark and skin-tone blending, and pigment work over areas of colour loss where it's appropriate.

Paramedical work is slow work. Skin that has been through surgery or injury behaves differently, so we assess the area, the scar's maturity and your tone before committing to a plan.

Work offered

  • Fine-line tattoo: Small, detailed designs drawn to your placement and skin.
  • Areola & 3D nipple restoration: Pigment and shading to rebuild a natural-looking areola after breast surgery.
  • Scar camouflage: Blending mature scars towards your surrounding skin tone.
  • Stretch-mark blending: Tone work across settled stretch marks to reduce contrast.
  • Skin-tone camouflage: Pigment matched to your surrounding skin for areas of colour loss, where suitable.
  • Cover & correction: Reworking older pigment or tattoo work where the skin and colour allow it.

How it works

  1. Consultation

    We look at the area, the scar or surgery history, your tone, and what's realistic.

  2. Patch test & design

    A pigment patch test, then a design or tone match you approve before we begin.

  3. Session

    Single-use needles, sterile setup, numbing where appropriate, worked at a pace the skin tolerates.

  4. Healing & review

    Aftercare, then a review session, and most paramedical work needs more than one pass.

Scars and surgical sites need to be fully healed and settled, usually a year or more, before pigment work. Bring your surgeon's clearance if you have one.
