What we treat

Five disciplines, one standard

Every treatment is preceded by a consultation — we read your skin and tone before we plan anything.

Skin

Facials, peels and skin therapy built around your actual skin type and its current state, not a fixed menu.

Hair

Scalp treatment and hair restoration therapy for thinning, fall, and texture and density concerns.

Laser

Hair reduction and skin resurfacing, calibrated to your tone before your first session begins.

Permanent Makeup

Brows, liner and lips — pigment matched and mapped to your features before a single pass is made.

Paramedical Tattoo

Areola, scar and skin-tone camouflage work for clients rebuilding after surgery, injury or illness.

Consultation

Every new client starts here — a skin and scalp read, a tone match, and a plan before anything is booked.
