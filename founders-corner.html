Founder's Corner

The standard starts with one pair of hands

Shookra was built around a single conviction: results you can see, without needles, and without rushing a client into a treatment she doesn't need.

Founder & Lead Practitioner

[Founder's Name] founded Shookra after years of treating skin, hair and pigment concerns across Delhi — and watching clients get sold quick fixes instead of a plan. Shookra was her answer: a certified studio on Shivalik Road where every client is read before she is treated, and where nothing is injected to get there.

She works across all five disciplines the studio offers — skin, hair, laser, permanent makeup and permanent tattoo — and teaches each of them at the academy. The students who train here learn on the same protocols her clients are treated with.

We don't use injectables. We aim to deliver results comparable to injectable treatments — naturally, and without needles.
