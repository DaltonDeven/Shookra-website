Shookra was built around a single conviction: results you can see, without needles, and without rushing a client into a treatment she doesn't need.

Founder's Story

Anya · Founder

Shookra was more than a dream; it was a vision brought to life. I wanted to create a space where beauty, confidence, comfort, and individuality come together.

For me, every person is unique and deserves to feel valued, cared for, and celebrated. At Shookra, every treatment is designed with love and attention to detail, creating an experience that leaves you feeling refreshed, confident, and empowered.

Shookra is more than an aesthetic clinic; it's also a space to learn, grow, and turn passion into a career in the beauty and aesthetics industry.

This is my journey of turning dreams into reality.

We don't use injectables. We aim to deliver results comparable to injectable treatments, naturally and without needles.

With love,
Anya 🤍
