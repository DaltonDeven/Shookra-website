Hair

Treat the scalp, and the hair follows

Hair fall is a symptom. We look at the scalp, the pattern and the history behind it before recommending a course of therapy.

Book a consultation

What we treat

Hair fall and shedding. Thinning through the crown or parting. Poor density and flat, lifeless texture. Dandruff, flaking, itch and product build-up. Scalps that stay oily or stay tight and dry.

Treatment here is topical and therapeutic, not surgical. We work on scalp health, circulation and the condition of the hair shaft, and we're honest when a concern is better seen by a doctor first.

Treatments offered

  • Scalp analysis — A close read of scalp condition, density and shedding pattern before any course is quoted.
  • Scalp detox & exfoliation — Clears build-up, flaking and congestion so treatment can actually reach the follicle.
  • Hair restoration therapy — A course of in-clinic sessions aimed at density and reduced fall, spaced over weeks.
  • Anti-dandruff protocol — For flaking, itch and recurring irritation, paired with a wash routine that holds it.
  • Hair spa & deep conditioning — Shaft repair for dryness, frizz and chemical or heat damage.
  • Nutrition & routine guidance — What to change at home between sessions — washing, heat, tying and diet basics.

How a course runs

  1. Consultation

    Pattern, duration, family history, diet, stress and recent illness — hair fall has causes worth naming.

  2. Scalp read

    We examine the scalp and density up close and set an honest expectation of what therapy can do.

  3. Course of sessions

    Treatment is spaced across weeks; one session doesn't change density.

  4. Review

    We reassess shedding and density before continuing, pausing or referring you on.

Sudden or patchy hair loss can be medical. If we see signs of that, we'll say so and suggest you see a doctor before starting a course.
