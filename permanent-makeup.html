Permanent Makeup

Mapped to your face, matched to your tone

Permanent makeup should look like your features on a good day, not like a template. Nothing is pigmented until the shape is drawn, agreed and sitting right on your face.

Book a consultation

What we do

Brows for sparse, over-plucked or uneven shapes. Eyeliner for definition that survives the day. Lip pigment for colour, shape and evenness. Correction work on faded or poorly placed previous work, where the existing pigment allows it.

Pigment is chosen against your undertone and your hair colour, and mapped to your bone structure and natural asymmetry, because no face is symmetrical, and good work respects that.

Treatments offered

  • Microblading & hair-stroke brows: Fine strokes that follow your natural growth direction, for a soft, realistic brow.
  • Powder & ombré brows: A softly shaded finish for oily skin or anyone who wants a filled-in look.
  • Eyeliner: From a fine lash enhancement between the lashes to a defined liner.
  • Lip blush & tint: Colour and definition that evens out lip tone and softens an uneven border.
  • Colour correction: Reworking faded, grey or misplaced pigment from previous work, where feasible.
  • Touch-up session: The follow-up that sets the final result after your skin has healed.

How it works

  1. Consultation & patch test

    Tone match, shape discussion, skin check and a pigment patch test.

  2. Mapping

    Your shape is drawn on and adjusted until you approve it in the mirror. Nothing starts before that.

  3. First pass

    Pigment is applied with numbing, in a single hygiene-controlled session.

  4. Healing & touch-up

    Colour softens as it heals. A touch-up after healing sets the final result.

Healed colour always looks softer than day one. Follow the aftercare, because picking or sun exposure during healing is what costs you pigment.
