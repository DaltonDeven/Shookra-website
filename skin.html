Skin

Skin work that starts with a read, not a menu

Two people with the same concern rarely need the same treatment. We read your skin type, its current condition and your history before we plan a single session.

Book a consultation

What we treat

Dullness, uneven tone and pigmentation. Active and post-acne skin. Open pores, congestion and texture. Early lines and loss of firmness. Sensitivity and barrier damage from over-treated skin.

Our approach is non-invasive by design. We don't use injectables; the plan is built from facials, peels, actives and in-clinic therapy, sequenced so your barrier stays intact while the skin improves.

Treatments offered

  • Clinical facials: Cleansing, extraction and active therapy chosen for your skin on the day, not booked blind.
  • Chemical peels: Strength and acid selected for your tone and sensitivity, built up gradually across a course.
  • Pigmentation therapy: Targeted protocols for melasma, sun damage and post-acne marks, with home care that holds the result.
  • Acne management: Congestion clearing plus a routine that reduces recurrence between sessions.
  • Hydration & barrier repair: For tight, reactive or over-exfoliated skin that needs rebuilding before anything else.
  • Firming & glow protocols: Non-injectable treatments aimed at tone, texture and lift before an event or as a course.

How a session runs

  1. Consultation

    Skin type, concerns, current products, medication and past treatments, all of it before anything is planned.

  2. Plan and patch

    You get a sequence, a realistic timeline, and a patch test where actives require one.

  3. Treatment

    Performed by a certified practitioner, adjusted in the chair if your skin responds differently that day.

  4. Home care and review

    A short routine you'll actually follow, then a review to decide the next session.

Pregnant or on prescribed skin medication? Tell us at consultation, since it changes which actives we can use.
