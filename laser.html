Laser

Calibrated to your tone before the first pass

Laser settings that suit one skin tone can burn another. Every course here begins with a tone assessment and a patch test. No exceptions.

Book a consultation

What laser can do here

Long-term hair reduction on the face and body. Resurfacing for texture, open pores and dullness. Support for pigmentation and post-acne marks as part of a wider skin plan.

Results build over a course, not a session. Hair grows in cycles, so treatment is spaced to catch it in the right phase, and we'll tell you at consultation roughly how many sessions your hair type is likely to need.

Treatments offered

  • Laser hair reduction: face: Upper lip, chin, jawline and sides, spaced to your growth cycle.
  • Laser hair reduction: body: Underarms, arms, legs, back and full-body courses.
  • Skin resurfacing: For texture, open pores and overall smoothness, planned alongside your skin routine.
  • Pigmentation support: Used as part of a skin plan where laser is appropriate for the concern.
  • Tone assessment & patch test: Done before your first session so settings are right for your skin, not generic.
  • Post-care guidance: Sun protection and aftercare that protects the result between sessions.

How a course runs

  1. Consultation

    Skin tone, hair type, medication, recent sun exposure and any past laser work.

  2. Patch test

    A small test area at the intended settings, then a wait before we proceed.

  3. Session

    Treatment at calibrated settings, with cooling and aftercare explained before you leave.

  4. Spaced sessions

    Repeat at the interval your growth cycle needs, reviewing reduction as we go.

Avoid waxing, threading and heavy sun exposure before a session. Tell us about any photosensitising medication at consultation.
